Pump Bug

Pump Bug is a device that watches an espresso machine and records each shot. It uses two signals: whether the pump is running (from an accelerometer) and how much is in the cup (from a Bluetooth scale). A shot is detected when both happen at once: the pump vibrating while coffee builds up in the cup. Click a box to open a short explanation of that part of the firmware.
